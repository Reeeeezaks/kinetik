# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First public version.

- Library of your own videos, GIFs and images plus Wallpaper Engine Workshop items (scenes, videos, web).
- Screen picker showing your real monitor layout with each screen's current wallpaper; multi-screen apply.
- Per-wallpaper properties with live preview: scaling, rotation, speed, volume (applied live).
- Discover: MotionBGs, MoeWalls and Steam Workshop, searched one at a time or all together.
- Playlists (on video end or timer, shuffle, crossfade).
- Global settings for pausing, frame rate, audio, blur, crossfade and more, pushed to all screens.
- Kolorful-style UI and a matching app icon.
